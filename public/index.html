    Know what you’re measuring Methodology & assumptions

    This is a historical, fixed-size long or short position, not a live trade or a trading strategy. Starting cash and base-asset size are independent inputs; the market determines the quote currency.

    Ending equity reflects the engine’s price PnL, signed funding, and reported fees. The chart plots returned equity samples; drawdown is the engine-reported peak-to-trough measure. Decimal amounts are preserved in JSON exports; chart values are converted to numbers for display.

    Check settlement gaps, index-derived marks, resynchronization, and candle coverage before interpreting results. Incomplete data can distort outcomes. Do not assume realistic execution, slippage, liquidation, or borrowing costs unless the engine explicitly reports them. Past performance does not predict future returns.
